# Authenticity Questions

Sent to every client — self-serve or concierge — as a voice-memo prompt.
The client records themselves answering these; that recording gets
transcribed and dropped in their Inbox like any other source, where
`voice-doc` mines it into their Voice document. This is the *general*
voice-gathering tool, distinct from `interview-questions-doc`'s output,
which is personalized per client and only used on camera with concierge
clients.

Formerly `personal-brand-pulse.md` — same artifact, expanded from 12
questions to this list and reframed from "Tait's own monthly check-in" to
"sent to any client, feeds their Voice document directly." Superseded by
this file; don't maintain both.

This list is expected to keep evolving. Treat it as versioned, not fixed —
note the date when it changes materially.

## The questions (as of 2026-08-24)

**Reflection — business and self**
1. What am I cutting from the business?
2. What's the lie the market is telling itself this month?
3. What did I learn from last week that I can't stop thinking about?
4. What is genuinely pissing me off right now that would be healthy to
   reflect on?
5. Where in my life are my standards too low right now?
6. What does a 10-out-of-10 brand look like for me this season?
7. What is a standard I've raised in the last 90 days?
8. What do I want to create over the next 90 days?
9. What's currently inspiring me?
10. What is my biggest dream to accomplish with the remainder of the year?
11. What does winning look like for me this week?

**Story-drawing — the ones aimed straight at the "story over value"
doctrine (see `story-over-value.md`)**
12. What is a story you could tell that recently taught you something?
13. Was there a trial you went through this week that reminded you of
    another time in your life you went through something similar?
14. What did I learn last week from a call that I couldn't stop thinking
    about?
15. What is a story from my life that prepared me for something I dealt
    with this past week?
16. What is some advice I recently received that really helped me?
17. What is something that kicked my butt this week?
18. What is a piece of advice you feel like the next generation needs to
    hear?

One question from the prior (12-question) version isn't in this dictation
and may just have been left out in the retelling, not deliberately cut:
*"Where am I currently making content where I should be making art?"* —
left out here rather than silently re-added; say the word if it should come
back in.

## Output

The voice memo becomes a transcript, saved into
`clients/<slug>/sources/<date>-authenticity-memo.txt` like any other raw
material. `voice-doc` mines it on the same pass as everything else in the
Inbox — no separate handling. Nothing about this list gets summarized down
to a theme before it's mined; the specific wording in the answer is what
makes it usable later.
