## The business

- Grad Gig is a marketplace connecting Wheaton, IL families/businesses with
  Wheaton College students for short-term gigs. (Legally, per the Terms of
  Service, eligibility is broader — any accredited college student, or a
  grad within the past 5 years — but the marketing and founder narrative are
  specifically Wheaton College. **Worth confirming with Luke** which is
  operationally true today.)
- Gig categories are much broader than "gutters and yard work" (the shorthand
  from the original brief): babysitting, overnight pet sitting, dog walking,
  moving/furniture assembly, yard work/lawn care, snow removal
  (request-based, not on-call), and tutoring across ~20 specific subjects
  (algebra through Spanish — see source file for the full list).
- Mechanism: a homeowner/business posts a gig and sets a rate → students in
  the Wheaton area apply → the customer approves a student → they coordinate
  and do the job directly → the student logs hours in the app → the
  customer's card is charged automatically → Grad Gig pays the student
  separately. Customer never pays the student directly; student never asks
  the customer for money.
- Money: Grad Gig charges the customer the student's rate **plus a 20%
  platform fee**, plus Stripe's card processing fee (~2.9%). It's free to
  join and browse.
- What students earn: roughly $20–$50/hour depending on the gig (per
  interview) — "pays higher than any on-campus job and most off-campus ones
  too" (site copy).
- Status: **live and operating**, not pre-launch — over 500 active users in
  the Wheaton community per the site's own founder bio. The Aug 24–Sep 18
  content plan is a growth/visibility push, not a from-zero launch. Content
  shouldn't frame this as brand new.
- Trust/safety constraint — **Grad Gig does not currently perform background
  checks** on students or customers (stated plainly in their own Terms).
  Content must never imply students are background-checked or formally
  vetted. The trust claim the platform actually stands behind is softer and
  values-based (see Positioning canvas below), not a verification claim.
- Growth ambition (from the founder bio): "to become the number one job
  provider for every Christian college in the nation" — currently Wheaton
  only.

## Founder facts

- Luke started Grad Gig as a senior at Wheaton College (site bio) and, per
  the live interview, has since graduated — both can be true, not a
  contradiction.
- Origin story (verbatim from the site, Luke's own framing): a tight class
  schedule and tight budget made a normal job incompatible with student
  life. He noticed Wheaton families constantly needed help (moving
  furniture, walking dogs, tutoring kids) and that students at Christian
  colleges bring something specific to the table: trust, character, hard
  work. Grad Gig was built to connect the two — "a win-win economy."
- What started as a small project connecting a few friends to local families
  has grown to 500+ active users.
- Voice/tone: not yet captured directly (the site copy is practical/FAQ-style,
  not necessarily how Luke talks in person) — open question, best answered
  from a call or voice memo rather than inferred from web copy.
- Non-negotiables: don't imply background checks/formal vetting (see above).
  Other do/don'ts still open.
