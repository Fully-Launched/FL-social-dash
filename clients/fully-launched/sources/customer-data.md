## 1. What Fully Launched does

Fully Launched helps people with digital media services, in four categories. **[Tait]**

1. **Social media** (Tait runs this). We help businesses and founders bring in new clients and more cash flow through content on social media.
2. **Websites.** We help redesign business websites so they bring in new customers.
3. **E-commerce / marketplaces.** We get people launched on Shopify, connect custom websites to Shopify and build out Shopify stores (with Claude in the back end). We also get people selling on Etsy, Amazon and TikTok Shop.
4. **AI systems.** We help business owners who need a custom Claude solution to their problems. *Example right now: a system for a business that automatically uploads files to a certain place, then Claude analyzes them, built as a Claude skill.*

**Platforms we post on right now:** LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook. **[Tait]**

## 2. Our unique solution

**This is the main thing this document exists to capture.**

**In plain words [Luke]:**
> We take one painful manual process or sales channel and ship a working solution in weeks, not months, and we build around how your business actually runs. It's not a template.

- We're **fast and fit for small operators** that the big agencies ignore. **[Luke]**
- **Start small and prove it works.** One process automated, or one channel done right, beats a big transformational project that changes everything and is overwhelming. **[Luke]**
- **We measure it** in hours saved or dollars kept. **[Luke]**
- **We simplify everything.** Your website gets simplified, your social media system gets simplified, so nothing is confusing. **[Tait]**

**By category:**

- **Social media: a whole content system in about 2 hours a month. [Tait]**
  1. The client records a voice memo (about 1 hour) that captures their unique ideas and their unique solutions to things. *"The exact thing I'm doing now is what I'm going to have clients do."*
  2. We turn it into content ideas and connect it with research on what type of content they should post.
  3. We build a whole schedule: what they need to film and when they need to film it by.
  4. The client films (about 1–2 hours).
  5. We post the content for them.
  6. We also set up their calls to action, email list, "all the stuff."
- **AI systems. [Tait]**
  > You're spending $60,000 a year on software that we can build you for $15,000, in a week, and you can have permanently.
  - We help people save a lot of money and time with AI systems.
  - A focused tool, small in scope and very specific, can cost just a few thousand. **[Luke]**
  - It can be one clean upload page and a confirm button. That's really all it is. **[Luke]**
- **Websites. [Tait]**
  - Custom websites that actually drive results, and don't just look cool.
  - Everyone can design a website with AI nowadays. Our edge is the strategy of making it simple: a simple, easy-to-use website.
- **Marketplaces. [Tait] [Luke]**
  - For the small business owner who wants to start selling online but doesn't know how. Examples: the person with an art shop, who we help get on Etsy, or the Etsy seller who wants to expand to their own custom Shopify store. **[Tait]**
  - We build custom stores so they can serve their customers. **[Tait]**
  - We check what nobody else checks, like whether the shipping settings lose money on every order. **[Luke]**

**Customer story [Luke]:** A customer had proven his business in lots of in-person markets but had very little online presence. We launched his brand across **Etsy, Walmart and Amazon**, and **doubled his sales in just a few months.**

## 5. Pain points

**Time and content (social media) [Tait]:**
- Don't have time.
- Don't know what to post.
- *"You sit there all day and think of what to post and just can't figure it out."*
- You constantly see people telling you what to post, and still can't figure out what you should be posting.
- Don't have time to film, and don't have time to create all the content.
- Don't know when to post or how to post.
- Can't stay consistent. *"Usually the problem is consistency."*
- **The balance:** building your own business *and* making content, with no time to do both.

**Running the business [Luke]:**
- Hours lost to **manual data entry**, like invoices, purchase orders and updating inventory.
- Products sell in person, but there's **no online sales channel.**
- Paying for **high-priced software** (ServiceTitan, Shopify, Faire) with **no integrator** to bring it all into one place.
- Their **photos, listings, product copy and marketing aren't good enough** for the places they're selling.
- They **don't know which marketplace is worth it.**
- **Fees for shipping and services are quietly eating their margin.**
- They've **tried hiring or agencies** that didn't come through with real fixes.
- The business needs them to be strategizing, but they're **doing clerical work** instead.

**[Brainstormed] More social media pain points, built from "no time" and "don't know what to post":**
- You post for a few weeks, see nothing happen, and quit.
- You know your customer better than any marketer, but you can't get it out of your head and onto video.
- Every idea you come up with feels generic, like something you've already seen a hundred times.
- Content is the first thing that gets dropped when the business gets busy.
- You film something, then never edit or post it.
- You don't know which posts are actually bringing in customers.
- You feel awkward on camera, so you put it off.
- Hiring a content person costs too much, or they don't understand your business.
- You have no plan, so you only post when you remember.
- You've tried using AI to write posts, and they sound nothing like you.

## 6. A bad day in the owner's life [Luke]

- They finish a full day running the business, then sit down at **8 PM to type 30 invoice line items** into their software.
- Or they're troubleshooting **a Faire order that shipped at a loss.**
- Or the business needs them to be strategizing, and they're **doing clerical work** that could be automated with AI, or solved by launching on new platforms and integrating systems so the business runs smoother.

**[Brainstormed] The social media version of a bad day:**
- It's 9 PM, the business is finally quiet, and you're staring at your phone trying to think of something to post.
- You haven't posted in three weeks, and a competitor just went viral with an idea you had months ago.

## 7. Fears

**Spoken [Luke]:**
- They'll **stay in the bottleneck forever.**
- **Competitors who figured out online sales or AI** integration and automation will outgrow them.
- They'll **spend a lot of money on tech that doesn't work**, or that they can't maintain or understand once the vendor disappears.
- They'll be **left stranded by agencies** that help them but don't teach them what they're using.
- **Fear of change.** Another platform might not be good, so they stick with the status quo.

**Unspoken: embarrassed or frustrated, but won't say it out loud [Luke]:**
- They don't really understand AI integrations, don't know how to do it, and **don't know where to start.** They feel behind on AI.
- They **should have started selling in a new place years ago**, and worry it's too late.
- Their real business **still runs on spreadsheets and their own memory**, not a real system.

**[Brainstormed] Social media fears:**
- Looking cringe on camera in front of people they know.
- Wasting money on content that doesn't bring in a single customer.
- Handing their voice to someone who makes them sound like everyone else.

- **Doing it all themselves**, then burning out on clerical work they don't need to do: work that could be automated, plus listings, ads, copy, AI photos, and custom software to take the business to the next level.

## 9. False beliefs, and the truth

| They believe | The truth |
|---|---|
| AI means a chatbot. **[Luke]** | Usually it means **removing one specific manual task.** It's not a chatbot that just agrees with you. It actually improves your business strategy and systems. **[Luke]** |
| Custom software costs six figures. **[Luke]** | A **focused, small-scope tool** can cost **just a few thousand.** **[Luke]** |
| More marketplaces means more sales. **[Luke]** | With bad or unoptimized listings, selling on five platforms **sells nothing more.** Get your product and copy right so it sells on each platform. **[Luke]** |
| I need to be technical to use this. **[Luke]** | It's **one clean upload page and a confirm button.** It's not complicated. It simplifies your current systems. **[Luke]** |
| AI should mass-produce my content, faster. **[Tait, from Reddit research]** | You can do that, but it's nowhere near as helpful as **using AI to get your own unique ideas out of your head**, organized and pushed out as content that solves your customer's needs. **[Tait]** |
| Going viral is about hooks and storytelling tricks. **[Reddit]** | It's really about **serving your audience and finding your unique perspective.** **[Tait]** |

## 10. Questions and objections we hear

**What we hear all the time [Luke]:**
- *"Can AI help with this?"*
- *"Will I have to maintain it?"*
- *"What if it breaks?"*
- *"Are the fees worth it?"*
- *"I don't have time to learn another platform."*
- *"We're too busy. We don't have time."*
- *"AI is not private."*

**What people ask on Reddit (their own words) [Reddit]:**
- *Social media and AI:* What do I post? Is there an AI system for this? How do I use AI to create content faster, at a mass-production level? How do you make content go viral? How do you come up with ideas in the first place? How do you know if an idea is actually good for posting? If you use AI, how do you prompt it? What type of content has actually brought you results? How do you keep a content calendar consistent when you're the only one running it? How do you grow in the era of AI-assisted content creation? Is CapCut still the easiest video editor for quick clips? How do you decide what content to post next?
- *Etsy:* Can someone critique my shop? Should I turn on Etsy ads? Why can't people find my Etsy shop? My new listings are getting zero views. *(One seller: what actually moved the needle in their first few months was focusing on the wedding niche. You can't appeal to everyone.)*
- *Shopify / e-commerce:* It costs more and more to acquire each customer while what they spend per order has barely changed, which slowly kills profitability. How do I get customer reviews? I started an herbal tea company and can't even get my first sale. Is embedded checkout worth switching to? What do you wish you'd figured out before starting your e-commerce store? Should I offer discounts? How should AI be used in e-commerce, and what AI tools speed up building an online store? How do I create a business website from scratch in one weekend without losing my mind?

## 11. What the industry gets wrong [Luke]

- Agencies love to sell **big retainers and AI strategy** instead of **shipping the one thing that works.**
- Marketplace agencies sell listings, but the listings **aren't profitable.** Nobody checks whether the shipping settings lose money on every order.
- **We start with one specific task or channel and measure it in hours saved or dollars kept.**

## 12. What we wish customers understood [Luke]

- We want to **start small, prove our process to you, and prove our value**, so you can see what we do actually works. One process automated or one channel done right beats a big transformational project that's overwhelming.
- **Your input is the biggest driver of the timeline:** photos, specs, pricing, access. The more organized and specific your instructions up front, the better job we can do.

## 13. Why we started Fully Launched [Luke]

- We kept seeing **great local businesses stuck because the owner was doing everything.**
- We kept seeing **online businesses fail because their Etsy and Faire listings weren't optimized** with good photos.
- We wanted to give businesses a helping hand, and to **work as a team with great guys on fast, fun projects.**
